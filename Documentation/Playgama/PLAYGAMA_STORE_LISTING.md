# Merge & Munch: Playgama store listing

## Title
Merge & Munch

## Game description
Drop, merge and munch! A cute bunny tray drops tasty snacks into a cozy pastel kitchen. Drop two of the same and
they merge into something bigger: a carrot and a carrot make a pear, and the menu keeps growing from there. Chain
merges for combos, beat your best score and climb the leaderboard. Easy to pick up and hard to put down. Just keep the
pile below the line!

## How to play
- **Aim:** drag your finger (or move the mouse) to slide the bunny tray left and right. The dashed guide shows where
  the food will land.
- **Drop:** release (or click) to drop the food.
- **Merge:** two identical foods that touch merge into a bigger one and score points. Quick chains score COMBOS.
- **Don't overflow:** keep the pile under the dashed line at the top. If food stays above it too long, the game ends.
- **Second chance:** out of room? Watch a short ad to keep your score and play on.

## Main category
Puzzle. Suggested tags: Merge, Casual, Physics, Food, Relaxing, Single player.

## Supported devices
Mobile (Android and iOS browsers), tablet and desktop. Touch and mouse controls.

## Screen orientation
Portrait. The layout also adapts to landscape on desktop.

## Game features
- Instant play: no menus, you are dropping food within the first second
- Satisfying physics merging with juicy pops, combos, score count-ups and confetti
- Drop guide that shows exactly where each piece will land
- Global best-score leaderboard with optional sign-in
- Rewarded "Continue" to keep a run going
- Best score and sound settings saved automatically
- Cozy pastel kitchen art, a cute bunny mascot, music and sound toggles
- Plays on phones, tablets and desktop
- Small and fast to load (about 20 MB)

English
